Sun and Moon Puzzle

Balance the cosmic forces in this logic game

How to Play

  • Fill the grid with suns (☀️) and moons (🌙).
  • Each row and column must contain exactly 3 suns and 3 moons.
  • No more than two identical symbols can be adjacent in a row or column.
  • The constraints between cells must be satisfied:
    • = means the symbols must be the same
    • × means the symbols must be different
  • Click on a cell to cycle through empty → sun → moon.
